Minister Track

Ministers represent the government in the Assembly. They do not sign the attendance register, ask questions, or introduce private member bills. They are scored separately on Probity and Legislative activity only.

Scoring Methodology

Based on internationally accepted frameworks:

  • IPU Indicators for Democratic Parliaments (SDG 16.6 & 16.7)
  • Sansad Ratna Awards criteria
  • PRS Legislative Research MLA Track methodology
  • B.PAC MLA Rating Framework

Four scoring dimensions:

  • Participation (30%) — Assembly attendance. IPU Target: Effective + Participatory.
  • Accountability (35%) — Questions raised (primary oversight tool). IPU Target: Accountable + Responsive.
  • Legislative Initiative (15%) — Private member bills + committee roles. IPU Target: Effective (Lawmaking).
  • Probity (20%) — Criminal record from election affidavits. IPU Target: Accountable (Ethics). Education deliberately excluded — it reflects background, not legislative performance.

Scores are computed using percentile ranking among peers. An MLA at the 80th percentile on a dimension scores 80 on that dimension.

Note: This scorecard measures legislative activity and integrity, not governance impact. An active MLA is not necessarily an effective one, and vice versa.
